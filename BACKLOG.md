## How to read this

- **Status** is the state in *our* code: `not_done`, `partial`, `done`, `unclear`. Each was checked against the code; file:line evidence is in the JSON.
- **Action**: `merge-pr` (PR applies as is), `cherry-pick` (take part of a PR), `adapt` (good idea, rewrite for current code), `adopt` (implement from the issue), `needs-decision`, `skip`.
- **Effort**: S ≤ half a day, M about 1–2 days, L more.
- Full detail per item (evidence, rationale, risk): [`docs/backlog/upstream-triage.json`](docs/backlog/upstream-triage.json).
